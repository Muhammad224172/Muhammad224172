## Muhammad Abdulrahman

MSc Data Science student at Sabancı University, Istanbul, working on machine learning for genomics. I am building Evoke, a genomic language model for zero-shot variant pathogenicity prediction, with Assist. Prof. Ogün Adebali.

### Research interests

- **Biological language modelling**: representation learning and tokenization strategies for genomic and protein sequences.
- **Variant effect prediction**: scalable methods for assessing the functional impact of genetic variation across the genome.
- **Discrete representations**: vector quantization and codebook learning for structured biological data.
- **Causal inference**: cause-and-effect relationships in high-dimensional biological and clinical data.
